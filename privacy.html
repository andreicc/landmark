Privacy Policy

Who is responsible

Landmark ("we") operates this website and is the controller of the personal data described below. Company identification details are set out on the Legal page. For anything in this policy, write to ioana@landmark.com.ro.

What we collect

  • Enquiry forms. First name, last name, email address, phone number, the residence type you select, and anything you write in the message field.
  • Consent. The fact that you ticked the consent box, and when.
  • Server logs. Our host records the usual request data, including your IP address, for security and reliability.

Why we use it, and on what basis

We use what you send solely to answer your enquiry and to arrange a viewing if you ask for one. The legal basis is your consent, given when you submit the form, and our legitimate interest in responding to someone who has contacted us. We do not use it for marketing unless you ask us to, and we do not sell or share it with third parties for their own purposes.

Who else can see it

  • Our hosting provider serves this site and keeps server logs.
  • Google Fonts and the Tailwind CDN serve typefaces and stylesheets. Your browser requests those files directly, so those providers can see your IP address. No account or cookie is involved.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry correspondence for as long as needed to deal with your request and any resulting sale, and then for the period required by Romanian accounting and tax law where a contract follows. If nothing comes of the enquiry, you can ask us to erase it at any time.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you may ask us for a copy of your data, ask us to correct or erase it, ask us to restrict or stop processing it, or ask for it in a portable format. You can withdraw consent at any time. Write to ioana@landmark.com.ro and we will respond within one month.

If you believe we have handled your data badly, you may complain to the Romanian supervisory authority, ANSPDCP.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date below. Material changes will be noted on this page.
